NOTES / RULES THAT APPLY THROUGHOUT
  - float64 throughout, including snapshots (dev 7.2)
  - Snapshots stream to parquet, never accumulate in memory (dev 7.3)
  - Exposures are not persisted; per-tick counts + 1% sample only
  - Registry stays a sorted list; never branch on exact float equality
  - Statistical reproducibility across seeds is the promise, not bitwise
  - Always sweep multiple seeds; report distributions, not points

DEVIATIONS
  2.3  Hawkes replies are a KERNEL TERM, not a scheduler. See the
       HawkesThreads docstring in dynamics/timing.py. Reproduces reply
       concentration on hot threads; does NOT reproduce within-thread
       burstiness, because a reply lands in whichever tick its exposure
       occurred in rather than at a drawn arrival time.
  2.2  long ties are drawn PROPORTIONAL TO PROMINENCE, not uniformly as
       the spec specifies. Introduced during calibration to stop the
       generator flattening the Pareto(2.3) prominence tail (in-degree
       alpha was 7.93). Shortcut property is preserved, but this is a
       deliberate departure from a spec clause that argues for uniform.
  3.1  cascades re-expose on the NEXT tick rather than in a within-tick
       wave loop. Documented in dynamics/tick.py.
  3.1  gumbel_argmax replaced by inverse-CDF sampling. Same distribution.
